**MudraNet: Viewpoint-Invariant Bharatanatyam Mudra Recognition Using Joint Graph Convolutional Networks**

MudraNet is a deep learning-based recognition system designed to identify and classify Bharatanatyam hand mudras using computer vision and Joint Graph Convolutional Networks (J-GCN). The project focuses on developing a viewpoint-invariant recognition approach that can accurately recognize traditional Bharatanatyam hand gestures even when they are captured from different viewing angles. By leveraging hand joint and skeletal information, the system analyzes the spatial relationships between different hand landmarks to distinguish various mudra patterns. The primary objective is to bridge the gap between traditional Indian classical dance and modern artificial intelligence by developing an intelligent, automated mudra recognition system that contributes to the digital preservation, learning, and understanding of Bharatanatyam.
